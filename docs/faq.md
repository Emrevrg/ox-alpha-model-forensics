# Frequently asked questions

## What is ox-alpha?

`ox-alpha` is the identifier used for a stealth model distributed through OpenRouter. A stealth listing exposes capabilities and behavior while withholding or delaying the developer/model identity.

## Why does the report favor GLM-5.3?

The assessment combines reported infrastructure traces, a 30/30 tokenizer-probe match, multimodal/video behavior, sixteen behavioral probes, and A/B comparison. The combination is more informative than any single item, but the conclusion remains an attribution hypothesis.

## What does "probable multimodal variant" mean?

The reports interpret vision/video behavior as evidence that the candidate may be a multimodal member or preview variant of the GLM-5.3 family. It is not presented as a confirmed official model name.

## How was the confidence calculated?

The reports describe an equal 12.5% baseline across eight candidate labs and a sequence of evidence updates ending near 93%. See the [Bayesian-style analysis](../methodology/bayesian-analysis.md). The number is assumption-dependent and should not be described as measured accuracy.

## Is this officially confirmed?

No. The project is an independent forensic assessment. Official confirmation would require a statement from the relevant platform or developer, or direct provenance evidence.

## Can I reproduce the result?

Only to the extent that the raw prompts, outputs, metadata, scoring rules, and comparison runs are published. Check the session and evidence manifests before claiming full reproduction.
